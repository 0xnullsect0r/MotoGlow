The current figure is the firmware's own estimator, not a decoration: it sums every channel of every pixel, scales by the 41.7 mA a full-white COB pixel draws at 12 V, and applies the brightness setting. The bar fills against the 2500 mA cap, and turns amber when the limiter engages and starts pulling the whole frame down. Chrome and Arctic Pulse will get there; Midnight never will.

Faithful: the segment positions, pixel counts, all eight motion primitives, the palettes, the speed and brightness tables, the 400 ms preset crossfade and the power-on sequence. Approximate: Ember's noise field is a different generator than FastLED's inoise8, and palettes interpolate continuously here rather than through 16 quantised entries. And a screen cannot show you what 500 mm of COB does to a fork leg at night.
